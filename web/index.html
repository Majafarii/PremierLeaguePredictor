A LITTLE CONTEXT

A model’s pick.
Not a promise.

This demo uses the same historical model as the project’s analysis. It can identify a lean, but football remains uncertain.

How does it choose a winner?+

Each team is evaluated separately as win versus non-win. A team is picked only when its win score is above 50% and its opponent’s is not. If neither passes, or both do, the page reports no clear winner. A non-win could be a draw or a loss.

Why is the data historical?+

The repository contains 2020–21 and partial 2021–22 match records, not a live feed. Every team uses its own last three recorded matches; the dates are shown above. Liverpool and some other teams have no 2021–22 team records. Matchups between different snapshots are illustrative.

How was the model evaluated?+

These are holdout classification results, not an accuracy guarantee for this hypothetical matchup tool. The displayed scores are uncalibrated model estimates, not verified probabilities.

Which inputs does it use?+

Venue, opponent, kickoff hour, weekday, and three-match averages of goals scored, goals conceded, shots, shots on target, shot distance, free kicks and penalties. Kickoff is fixed to Saturday at 15:00 for this basic demo.

Training windows use closed='left' to exclude each current match. For a hypothetical next fixture, all three most recent completed matches are already known and may be used.
